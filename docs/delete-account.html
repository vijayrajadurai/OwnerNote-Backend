Delete your OwnerNote account

Last updated: 24 October 2026
App: OwnerNote (Android package com.ownernote.app)
Publisher: NewonX

This page is for the OwnerNote app from NewonX. You can request deletion without opening the app and without signing in.

Steps to request deletion

  1. Email supportownernote@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Delete OwnerNote account.
  3. In the message, write the mobile number you used to sign in to OwnerNote (the number that received the OTP).
  4. We reply to that email to confirm the request. You do not need a password.

What we delete

Within 30 days after we confirm the request, we delete these from our servers:

  • Your account, phone number, and sign-in tokens.
  • Shop profile: owner name, shop name, category, city, pincode, year started, approximate monthly income, and shop location.
  • Customers and suppliers, including a name and phone number you chose from your phone contacts.
  • Credit, debit, payments, bills, stock, reminders, and other business records stored for that account.
  • The push-notification token tied to that account.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Backup copies of the deleted account are removed within the same 30 days.
  • We keep a record only when the law requires it, and only for the period the law requires.
  • Data that stays only on your phone (captured bill images, notes, and on-device books) is removed when you uninstall OwnerNote or clear the app’s storage. We cannot erase your phone’s own contact list.

We do not offer deletion of only some server data while the account stays open. The request above deletes the account and the server data listed here.
