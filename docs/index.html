OwnerNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026
App: OwnerNote (Android package com.ownernote.app)
Publisher: NewonX

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the OwnerNote mobile application. It explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use OwnerNote. You can also read this policy inside the app (More → Privacy Policy, Settings, and Subscription). This page is a standard, public webpage. It is not a PDF and does not require a login.

Delete your account

To delete your OwnerNote account and the data we store for it, follow the steps on Delete your OwnerNote account. You do not need to open the app or sign in.

  1. Email supportownernote@gmail.com with the subject Delete OwnerNote account.
  2. Include the mobile number you used to sign in (the OTP number).

Within 30 days after we confirm the request, we delete the account, phone number, shop profile, customers and suppliers (including a contact name and number you chose), and the business records stored on our servers. Backup copies are removed in that same 30 days. We keep a record only when the law requires it, for the period the law requires. Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall OwnerNote or clear app storage. We do not erase your phone’s own contact list. We do not delete only some server data while the account stays open.

1. Who we are

OwnerNote is a shop-management app for business owners in India. It helps you keep books, scan bills, track stock, set reminders, and use the Kai assistant. “We”, “us”, and “the app” mean NewonX acting as the provider of OwnerNote.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app and which permissions you grant, we may process:

  • Account identifiers: mobile phone number, one-time password (OTP), and sign-in tokens used to create and verify your account.
  • Shop profile: owner name, shop name, business category, city, pincode, year the business started, approximate monthly income, and optional shop location (precise location if you tap “Use shop location”).
  • Business records you enter or scan: customers, suppliers, credit and debit, daily cash, inventory, bills, handwritten notes, voice entries, payment status, and reminders (including names, amounts, dates, and photos or text from documents you capture).
  • Contacts you choose: if you allow contacts, a contact you pick is read for its name and phone number and can be saved as a customer or supplier. We do not upload your whole address book.
  • Device and app data: app language and theme, reminder and notification settings, crash logs, and a push-notification token (FCM) so we can deliver alerts on this device.
  • Permissions you choose to allow: camera (bills and notes), microphone (voice entry and check-in), location (group buying / shop pin), contacts (the contact you pick), and notifications.

We do not require this information from children. OwnerNote is for shop owners, not for children.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account (phone number and OTP sign-in).
  • Run OwnerNote features: ledger, billing, inventory, reminders, Kai, group buying, offers, reports, and support.
  • Match nearby shops in your category for group-buying requests. Exact map pins are not shown to other shops.
  • Send reminders, payment alerts, and optional daily voice check-in if you enable them.
  • Diagnose crashes and keep the app reliable.

We do not sell your shop data. We do not use your business records for third-party advertising. Account, shop, contact, and business data sent off the phone is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

4. Where information is stored

Some data stays on your phone (for example captured bill images, handwritten notes, and on-device books). Account and business data may also be stored on our application servers (API) so the app can function. Crash and authentication services store limited technical data as described below.

5. How we share information

We share information only with parties that help us operate OwnerNote:

  • Google Firebase / Google LLC: phone authentication, cloud messaging (notifications), analytics/crash reporting as configured in the app.
  • Our API host: servers that store and return your OwnerNote account and business data (currently hosted with our cloud provider for the OwnerNote backend).
  • Google Play: if you buy an optional subscription later, Google processes the payment. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Legal requests: we may disclose information if required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

Other shops in group buying may see your shop name and interest in a deal. They do not see your exact map pin, phone OTP, or full ledger.

6. Retention

We keep account and business data while your account is active and as needed to provide the app. After you request account deletion and we confirm it, server data listed on the deletion page is deleted within 30 days, including backups. Device-only data is removed if you uninstall the app or clear app storage. Logout clears local captured bills, notes, and reminder state on that phone for the next account.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Refuse camera, microphone, location, contacts, or notification permission. Related features will not work until you allow them.
  • Change language and notification settings in the app.
  • Log out of the account on this device.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated server data by the steps at the top of this policy, or on Delete your OwnerNote account.

8. Security

We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS) for network traffic and platform sign-in (Firebase OTP). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Keep your phone locked and do not share OTP codes.

9. International access

This policy webpage is intended to be available worldwide without a login or geographic block. Service providers (including Google) may process data in the countries where they operate.

10. Children

OwnerNote is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page and in the in-app policy.
